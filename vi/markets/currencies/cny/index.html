Chinese Yuan (Renminbi)

The currency of the world's second-largest economy, managed within a band by the PBOC. Traded onshore (CNY) and offshore (CNH) at slightly different prices.

History

Pegged to the dollar for decades, the yuan moved to a managed float in 2005. The surprise 2015 devaluation shook global markets and previewed its growing systemic weight.
