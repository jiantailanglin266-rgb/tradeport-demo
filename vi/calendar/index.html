Lịch Kinh Tế

Những công bố theo lịch làm dịch chuyển tiền tệ — thời điểm theo múi giờ của bạn, mỗi chỉ số đo lường gì, và vì sao thị trường phản ứng.

Cách các ngày này được tạo ra: ngày họp ngân hàng trung ương lấy trực tiếp từ lịch chính thức. Các chỉ số có quy tắc công bố (ví dụ bảng lương Mỹ vào thứ Sáu đầu tiên) được tính từ quy tắc đó. Những công bố có ngày thay đổi hằng tháng được liệt kê không kèm ngày — hãy theo liên kết lịch chính thức. Chúng tôi không phỏng đoán ngày.

Tra cứu chỉ số

Mỗi công bố thực sự đo lường điều gì, vì sao nó dịch chuyển thị trường, và cặp tiền nào chịu tác động nhất.

USD

🇺🇸

Non-Farm Payrolls (NFP)

Tác động cao
Đo lường điều gì
The net change in paid US jobs outside farming, private households and non-profits, released with the unemployment rate and average hourly earnings.
Vì sao thị trường dịch chuyển
Employment drives the Fed's policy path. A large surprise repricess rate expectations within seconds, and the wage component often matters more than the headline because it feeds inflation.
Chu kỳ công bố
Monthly, usually the first Friday, 08:30 New York time.
USD/JPYEUR/USDXAU/USD

U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics · Lịch chính thức

🇺🇸

Initial Jobless Claims

Tác động trung bình
Đo lường điều gì
The number of people filing for unemployment benefits for the first time in the past week.
Vì sao thị trường dịch chuyển
The most frequent read on the US labour market. Single weeks are noisy, so traders watch the four-week average for turning points ahead of the monthly payrolls report.
Chu kỳ công bố
Weekly, Thursdays, 08:30 New York time.
USD/JPYEUR/USD

U.S. Department of Labor · Lịch chính thức

🇺🇸

Consumer Price Index (CPI)

Tác động cao
Đo lường điều gì
The change in prices paid by US urban consumers. Core CPI strips out food and energy to show the underlying trend.
Vì sao thị trường dịch chuyển
In an inflation-fighting cycle CPI is the single most important release for the dollar, because it directly shapes how quickly the Fed can cut or must hike.
Chu kỳ công bố
Monthly, typically mid-month. The exact date changes each month — see the official BLS schedule.
USD/JPYEUR/USDXAU/USD

U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics · Lịch chính thức

🇺🇸

Core PCE Price Index

Tác động cao
Đo lường điều gì
The Fed's preferred inflation gauge, measuring prices of personal consumption expenditure excluding food and energy.
Vì sao thị trường dịch chuyển
The Fed targets PCE, not CPI. When the two diverge, PCE is the one policy actually responds to — which is why a quiet CPI can still be followed by a market-moving PCE print.
Chu kỳ công bố
Monthly, usually late in the month. Check the BEA release schedule.
USD/JPYEUR/USD

U.S. Bureau of Economic Analysis · Lịch chính thức

🇺🇸

US GDP (quarterly)

Tác động trung bình
Đo lường điều gì
The total value of goods and services produced in the US, released in advance, second and third estimates.
Vì sao thị trường dịch chuyển
It is backward-looking, so the reaction is usually smaller than for CPI or payrolls — but a sharp miss changes the recession narrative and with it the expected rate path.
Chu kỳ công bố
Quarterly, with three revisions per quarter. See the BEA schedule.

US Retail Sales

Tác động trung bình
Đo lường điều gì
The change in the total value of sales at the retail level, including a 'control group' used in GDP.
Vì sao thị trường dịch chuyển
Consumption is roughly two thirds of the US economy, so retail sales is the fastest read on whether the consumer is still carrying growth.
Chu kỳ công bố
Monthly, usually mid-month. See the Census release calendar.
USD/JPYEUR/USD

ISM Manufacturing PMI

Tác động trung bình
Đo lường điều gì
A survey of purchasing managers in manufacturing. Above 50 signals expansion, below 50 contraction.
Vì sao thị trường dịch chuyển
A forward-looking survey released early in the month, so it often sets the tone before the hard data arrives. The prices-paid sub-index is watched as an inflation signal.
Chu kỳ công bố
Monthly, on the first business day, 10:00 New York time.

🇺🇸

ISM Services PMI

Tác động cao
Đo lường điều gì
A survey of purchasing managers in US service industries, which make up roughly four-fifths of the economy.
Vì sao thị trường dịch chuyển
Services carry far more of US output and employment than manufacturing, so this print says more about recession risk than the factory survey. The prices-paid and employment sub-indices are read as early inflation and payroll signals.
Chu kỳ công bố
Monthly, on the third business day. The exact date varies — see the ISM schedule.
USD/JPYEUR/USD

Institute for Supply Management · Lịch chính thức

🇺🇸

Producer Price Index (PPI)

Tác động trung bình
Đo lường điều gì
The change in prices US producers receive for their output, before those costs reach consumers.
Vì sao thị trường dịch chuyển
PPI usually lands a day or two after CPI and contains the components that feed directly into the Fed's preferred PCE gauge, so it lets traders refine the PCE forecast before the official release.
Chu kỳ công bố
Monthly, usually shortly after CPI. The exact date changes each month.

U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics · Lịch chính thức

🇺🇸

ADP National Employment Report

Tác động trung bình
Đo lường điều gì
Private-sector job creation estimated from the payroll records ADP processes for over 25 million US workers.
Vì sao thị trường dịch chuyển
It lands two days before the official payrolls report and is traded as a preview, even though its correlation with the government figure is loose. Expect a knee-jerk move that often reverses on Friday.
Chu kỳ công bố
Monthly, usually the Wednesday before the payrolls report.

JOLTS Job Openings

Tác động trung bình
Đo lường điều gì
The number of unfilled job openings on the last business day of the reference month, with hires and quits.
Vì sao thị trường dịch chuyển
The openings-to-unemployed ratio is one of the Fed's stated measures of labour-market tightness, and the quits rate is treated as a leading indicator of wage growth.
Chu kỳ công bố
Monthly, released with roughly a two-month lag.

University of Michigan Consumer Sentiment

Tác động trung bình
Đo lường điều gì
A household survey of confidence in personal finances and the economy, published with 1-year and 5-10 year inflation expectations.
Vì sao thị trường dịch chuyển
The inflation-expectation series is the part that moves markets: Fed officials cite it directly, and an unanchored long-run reading raises the odds of tighter policy.
Chu kỳ công bố
Monthly, a preliminary reading mid-month and a final reading near month-end.

CB Consumer Confidence

Tác động trung bình
Đo lường điều gì
A survey of how households see business and labour-market conditions now and six months ahead.
Vì sao thị trường dịch chuyển
Its labour-differential question — jobs 'plentiful' minus 'hard to get' — tracks the unemployment rate closely, so it is used as an early read on the payrolls report.
Chu kỳ công bố
Monthly, usually the last Tuesday of the month.

Durable Goods Orders

Tác động trung bình
Đo lường điều gì
New orders placed with US manufacturers for goods meant to last three years or more.
Vì sao thị trường dịch chuyển
The headline swings wildly on aircraft orders. Traders watch core capital goods orders excluding aircraft and defence, which is the cleanest monthly read on business investment.
Chu kỳ công bố
Monthly, around the end of the month.

U.S. Census Bureau · Lịch chính thức

🇺🇸

EIA Crude Oil Inventories

Tác động trung bình
Đo lường điều gì
The weekly change in US commercial crude oil stocks, with gasoline and distillate stocks and refinery runs.
Vì sao thị trường dịch chuyển
The single biggest scheduled mover of oil prices, which in turn drives the Canadian dollar and the energy component of inflation. Holiday weeks push the release to Thursday.
Chu kỳ công bố
Weekly, Wednesdays 10:30 New York time (Thursday after a Monday holiday).

U.S. Energy Information Administration · Lịch chính thức

🇺🇸

CFTC Commitments of Traders

Tác động trung bình
Đo lường điều gì
Aggregated futures positioning by category of trader, as of the previous Tuesday.
Vì sao thị trường dịch chuyển
It rarely moves price on release, but it is the standard public gauge of how crowded a trade is. Extreme speculative positioning is what makes a currency vulnerable to a sharp unwind.
Chu kỳ công bố
Weekly, Fridays 15:30 New York time, covering positions as of Tuesday.

Eurozone HICP (flash estimate)

Tác động cao
Đo lường điều gì
The harmonised measure of consumer price inflation across the euro area.
Vì sao thị trường dịch chuyển
The ECB's mandate is price stability, so HICP is the primary input to its rate decisions. National releases from Germany and Spain the day before often front-run the euro-area figure.
Chu kỳ công bố
Monthly flash estimate, at the end of the month.

Eurozone Flash PMI

Tác động cao
Đo lường điều gì
First estimate of purchasing-manager activity in eurozone manufacturing and services, with French and German breakdowns released first.
Vì sao thị trường dịch chuyển
The earliest monthly read on the euro-area cycle, published before the official data. The German and French prints land minutes earlier and often set the direction before the bloc-wide number.
Chu kỳ công bố
Monthly, around the 23rd. National releases precede the eurozone figure.

German Ifo Business Climate

Đo lường điều gì
A survey of about 9,000 German firms on current conditions and expectations for the next six months.
Vì sao thị trường dịch chuyển
Germany is the largest euro-area economy, and the expectations component has a long record as a turning-point signal for the whole bloc's industrial cycle.
Chu kỳ công bố
Monthly, around the 25th.

🇩🇪

German ZEW Economic Sentiment

Tác động trung bình
Đo lường điều gì
A survey of financial analysts and institutional investors on the six-month outlook for the German economy.
Vì sao thị trường dịch chuyển
Because it polls market professionals rather than firms, it reacts to news faster than Ifo and is read as the most forward-looking of the German sentiment surveys.
Chu kỳ công bố
Monthly, usually the second or third Tuesday.
EUR/USDEUR/JPY

The first estimate of quarterly euro-area economic growth, published about 30 days after the quarter ends.

It confirms or breaks the narrative the PMIs have been building all quarter. A negative print revives recession pricing and shifts ECB cut expectations.

Đo lường điều gì
The change in consumer prices in the United Kingdom, including core and services inflation.

Services inflation and wage growth are what the Bank of England has focused on, so the sub-components frequently move sterling more than the headline rate.

UK Labour Market Report

Tác động cao
Đo lường điều gì
Unemployment, employment change and average weekly earnings, including the closely watched regular pay excluding bonuses.
Vì sao thị trường dịch chuyển
Wage growth is the Bank of England's central concern for inflation persistence. The pay figure moves sterling more reliably than the unemployment rate does.
Chu kỳ công bố
Monthly, usually the Tuesday of the second or third week, 07:00 London time.

🇬🇧

UK Monthly GDP

Tác động trung bình
Đo lường điều gì
Monthly economic output, published with industrial production, construction and the trade balance.
Vì sao thị trường dịch chuyển
The UK is one of the few major economies publishing GDP monthly, so the growth picture updates twelve times a year instead of four — and sterling reprices with it.
Chu kỳ công bố
Monthly, around six weeks after the reference month, 07:00 London time.

UK Retail Sales

Đo lường điều gì
The change in the volume of goods sold by UK retailers, adjusted for price changes.
Vì sao thị trường dịch chuyển
UK growth is consumption-led, and the volume basis means it captures whether households are actually buying more rather than just paying more.

Monthly, usually a Friday around three weeks after the month ends.

Japan CPI / Tokyo CPI

Tác động trung bình
Đo lường điều gì
Japanese consumer price inflation. The Tokyo area figure is released about three weeks ahead of the nationwide number.

After decades of deflation, sustained Japanese inflation is what allows the Bank of Japan to normalise policy — which is the central question for the yen.

A survey of Chinese manufacturers; the official NBS index and the private Caixin index are both watched.

China is the demand engine for industrial commodities, so the print moves the Australian and New Zealand dollars during Asian hours as much as it moves the yuan.

Chinese exports, imports and the resulting trade surplus, in both dollar and yuan terms.
Vì sao thị trường dịch chuyển
Imports are the part that matters for the Australian dollar, because they show whether Chinese demand for iron ore and other commodities is holding up. The release time is not fixed.
Chu kỳ công bố
Monthly, around the 7th to the 15th. The exact time is not announced in advance.

The Reserve Bank of Australia's policy rate decision and accompanying statement.
Vì sao thị trường dịch chuyển
The Australian dollar is one of the most rate-sensitive majors because of its role in carry trades. The statement's guidance usually matters more than the rate itself.
Chu kỳ công bố
Eight meetings a year. Dates are published on the RBA site; we do not reproduce them here because the RBA does not publish them in a machine-readable form.

The full-time/part-time split matters more than the headline: a large gain made up entirely of part-time work does not tighten the labour market the way the number suggests.

Consumer price inflation, published both as a monthly indicator and as the fuller quarterly series.

The RBA weights the quarterly trimmed mean most heavily, so the quarterly release moves the Australian dollar considerably more than the monthly indicator does.

Monthly indicator plus a quarterly release, 11:30 Sydney time.

Vì sao thị trường dịch chuyển
The BoC targets its own trim and median series rather than the headline, so those two numbers decide the reaction even when the headline surprises.

Monthly, around the middle of the month, 08:30 Toronto time.

SNB Monetary Policy Assessment

Tác động cao
Đo lường điều gì
The Swiss National Bank's quarterly policy decision and conditional inflation forecast.
Vì sao thị trường dịch chuyển
The SNB meets only four times a year, so each decision carries a full quarter of accumulated expectations. Comments on currency intervention move the franc as much as the rate does.
Chu kỳ công bố
Quarterly — March, June, September and December, 09:30 Zurich time.

Swiss CPI

Tác động trung bình
Đo lường điều gì
Swiss consumer price inflation, historically among the lowest in the developed world.
Vì sao thị trường dịch chuyển
With inflation usually near zero, small deviations carry a lot of information about whether the SNB needs to lean against franc strength.

Monthly, in the first days of the month, 08:30 Zurich time.

RBNZ Official Cash Rate

Tác động cao
Đo lường điều gì
The Reserve Bank of New Zealand's policy rate decision, with a full Monetary Policy Statement at alternate meetings.
Vì sao thị trường dịch chuyển
The RBNZ publishes its own forecast track for the cash rate. A shift in that projected path moves the New Zealand dollar more than the decision itself.
Chu kỳ công bố
Seven meetings a year, four with a full Monetary Policy Statement.
