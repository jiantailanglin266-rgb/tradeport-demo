Tổng Quan Chiến Lược

Nova NightOwl trades the low-volatility window after the New York close, fading small deviations back to a short-term mean on USD/CAD and EUR/CHF.

It stands aside during rollover minutes when spreads spike, and stops trading each night after either the profit or loss cap is reached.

Cài Đặt & Hướng Dẫn

  1. 1Requires an account with low, stable night spreads — verify yours first.
  2. 2Attach to M15 charts; set GMT offset precisely.
  3. 3Night strategies degrade if spreads widen: monitor monthly.
