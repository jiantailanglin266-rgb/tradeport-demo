Position Sizing: Turning Risk Percent into Lot Size

Position sizing converts an abstract risk rule into a concrete lot size for every trade. Walk through the formula, pip values, and worked examples for FX pairs and gold.

Tác Giả: M. RiveraKiểm duyệt bởi: H. SuzukiNgày đăng: 24 thg 5, 2026Cập nhật: 8 thg 7, 2026Dữ liệu kiểm tra lần cuối: 8 thg 7, 2026

Điểm Chính

  • ✓Size = (equity × risk%) ÷ (stop distance in pips × pip value).
  • ✓Pip value depends on the pair, lot size and your account currency.
  • ✓The stop distance comes from the chart first; size is always derived.
  • ✓Volatile instruments get smaller size for identical dollar risk.
  • ✓Recalculate as equity changes — sizing is dynamic, not set-and-forget.

Mục Lục

  1. 1. What is the position sizing formula?
  2. 2. How do pip values differ across instruments?
  3. 3. Should size adjust to volatility?
  4. 4. Câu Hỏi Thường Gặp

What is the position sizing formula?

Lot size = (equity × risk fraction) ÷ (stop distance × pip value per lot). With $10,000 equity, 1% risk, a 50-pip stop on EUR/USD where one standard lot is $10 per pip: size = $100 ÷ (50 × $10) = 0.20 lots.

The same $100 risk with a 25-pip stop allows 0.40 lots; with a 100-pip stop only 0.10. Dollar risk stays constant while size adapts to the trade's structure — this is the entire point.

How do pip values differ across instruments?

For pairs quoted in USD (EUR/USD, GBP/USD) a standard lot is $10 per pip. For yen pairs pip value floats with USD/JPY. Gold is typically quoted so that $0.10 of price movement equals $10 per 100-oz lot — check your broker's contract specification.

When your account currency differs from the quote currency, convert at current rates. Most platforms and EA risk inputs handle this automatically, but verify once by hand so you can spot configuration errors.

Should size adjust to volatility?

Yes — indirectly, through the stop. Volatile conditions demand wider stops (often set via ATR), which automatically reduce size for the same dollar risk. This keeps every trade's real risk equal regardless of the instrument's temperament.

Avoid the common inversion: keeping a fixed lot size and widening stops in volatility. That silently multiplies dollar risk exactly when conditions are most dangerous.

Câu Hỏi Thường Gặp

›What is the difference between standard, mini and micro lots?

A standard lot is 100,000 units of base currency, a mini 10,000, a micro 1,000. Micro lots let small accounts size precisely to a 1% risk rule.

›How does this apply to EAs?

Good EAs expose risk-percent or fixed-money inputs and compute lots from the stop internally. Prefer those over fixed-lot settings, and verify with a small forward test.

›Do I resize after each win or loss?

With fixed fractional sizing, size follows current equity — growing after wins, shrinking after losses. That asymmetry is a feature: it compounds gains and slows drawdowns.
