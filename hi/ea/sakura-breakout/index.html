रणनीति का सारांश

Sakura Breakout measures the Tokyo session range and places stop orders on both sides ahead of the London open, cancelling the opposite order on first fill.

Stops are placed beyond the range midpoint, scaled by recent ATR. All positions close before the New York session ends — no overnight exposure.
