Resumen de la Estrategia

Aurora Trend detects momentum shifts using a dual moving-average structure confirmed by ADX strength. It enters on pullbacks in the trend direction, never chasing breakouts.

Each position carries a hard stop-loss from entry, sized by ATR. A trailing stop locks in profit as the trend extends. The news filter pauses trading around high-impact releases.

Configuración e Instalación

  1. 1Copy the EA into your platform's Experts folder and restart (see our installation guide).
  2. 2Attach to EUR/USD H1 chart. Defaults are tuned for this pair.
  3. 3Set RiskPercent to 1.0 or below for the first month.
  4. 4Enable the news filter and set your broker's GMT offset.
  5. 5Run on demo for at least 4 weeks before live deployment.

VPS Recomendado: 1 vCPU / 2GB RAM, latency < 50ms to broker
