Enciclopedia de Historia Financiera

Del Lunes Negro al shock del franco suizo — comprende los eventos que formaron los mercados de hoy.

  1. 1929Oct 1929CRISIS

    The Great Depression

    The 1929 Wall Street crash triggered a decade-long global depression, bank failures and the collapse of the gold standard.

    Impact: Reshaped monetary policy forever: deposit insurance, securities regulation and the modern central bank's lender-of-last-resort role all trace back to this collapse.

  2. 1971Aug 1971POLICY

    The Nixon Shock — End of Bretton Woods

    The US suspended dollar-gold convertibility, ending fixed exchange rates and giving birth to the floating FX market traders know today.

    Impact: Without this event there is no retail forex: every floating currency pair, every FX trend and every currency crisis since 1973 flows from this decision.

  3. 1985Sep 1985POLICY

    The Plaza Accord

    G5 nations agreed to coordinate a weakening of the dollar; the yen roughly doubled in value within three years.

    Impact: The definitive proof that coordinated intervention can move currencies for years — and a founding chapter of Japan's bubble economy and its aftermath.

  4. 1987Oct 19, 1987CRISIS

    Black Monday

    The Dow fell 22.6% in a single day — still the largest one-day percentage crash — amplified by early program trading.

    Impact: Gave markets circuit breakers and gave central banks the 'Fed put' reflex: the promise of liquidity support that has shaped every crisis response since.

  5. 1997Jul 1997CRISIS

    The Asian Financial Crisis

    The collapse of the Thai baht's dollar peg cascaded through Asia, crushing currencies and forcing IMF rescues across the region.

    Impact: Taught emerging markets to hoard FX reserves and float their currencies — and taught traders that pegged exchange rates are one-way option bets waiting to break.

  6. 2000Mar 2000MARKET

    The Dot-Com Bubble

    Internet-stock mania pushed the Nasdaq up fivefold in five years before a 78% collapse erased trillions.

    Impact: The modern template for narrative bubbles: revolutionary technology, real long-term impact — and devastating losses for those who confused the two timelines.

  7. 2008Sep 2008CRISIS

    The Global Financial Crisis

    The US housing collapse and Lehman Brothers' failure froze global credit and triggered the deepest recession since the 1930s.

    Impact: Created the world traders inhabit now: zero rates, quantitative easing, coordinated central bank dominance of markets, and the modern regulatory architecture.

  8. 2015Jan 15, 2015MARKET

    The Swiss Franc Shock

    The SNB abandoned its 1.20 EUR/CHF floor without warning; the pair fell ~20% in minutes, bankrupting brokers and wiping out accounts.

    Impact: FX's defining lesson in gap risk: stop losses cannot protect against discontinuous prices, negative balances are real, and broker solvency is part of your risk.
