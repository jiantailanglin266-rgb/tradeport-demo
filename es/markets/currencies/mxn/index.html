Mexican Peso

The most traded Latin American currency and a global carry favorite when Mexican rates run high. Liquid for an EM currency, but gaps hard in risk-off.

País Emisor
Mexico
Banco Central
Banco de México (Banxico)
Sesiones Principales
New York 13:00–22:00 GMT
Código
MXN

Notas de Trading

  • ▹EM carry trade staple
  • ▹US economy linkage (trade, remittances)
  • ▹Liquid in NY hours
  • ▹Sharp risk-off drawdowns

Factores Clave

Banxico rate pathUS economic cycleOil pricesEM risk sentimentUS trade policy

History

The 1994 Tequila Crisis forced a float and an international rescue. Decades of orthodox policy since have made the peso the EM benchmark for carry strategies.
