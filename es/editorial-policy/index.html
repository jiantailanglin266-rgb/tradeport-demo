Política Editorial

The rules every TRADEPORT guide, review and comparison follows.

Actualizado: 1 jul 2026

Accuracy and sourcing

Claims about regulation, pricing and platform features must trace to a primary source (regulator registries, official documentation) and carry a data-checked date. Where we cannot verify, we label the item 'Verification Required' rather than present it as fact.

Independence

Affiliate relationships fund the site but do not touch editorial output. Writers and reviewers do not know payout terms when scoring brokers. Rankings are produced by the published methodology, and any sponsored placement would be labeled as advertising.

Risk-first writing

Every product page must document risks with the same prominence as benefits. Content that promises profits, hides drawdowns or downplays leverage risk fails review and is not published.

Advertencia de Riesgo: Operar FX y CFDs con apalancamiento implica un alto riesgo de perder dinero. Nunca operes con fondos que no puedas permitirte perder.
