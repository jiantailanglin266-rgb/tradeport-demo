Canadian Dollar

An oil-linked G10 currency trading in near-lockstep with US economic cycles. USD/CAD is the cleanest crude-oil expression in major FX.

Negara Penerbit
Canada
Bank Sentral
Bank of Canada (BOC)
Sesi Perdagangan Utama
New York 13:00–22:00 GMT
Kode
CAD

Catatan Trading

  • ▹Strong oil price correlation
  • ▹Tied to US economic cycle
  • ▹Liquid mainly in NY hours
  • ▹BOC often moves near the Fed

Faktor Penggerak Utama

Crude oil (WTI)BOC decisionsCanadian employment & CPIUS data spillover

History

The 'loonie' floated in 1970, earlier than most peers. Energy booms and busts — from oil sands expansion to the 2014 crash — define its long cycles.
