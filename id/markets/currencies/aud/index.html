Australian Dollar

The classic commodity currency: closely tied to iron ore, coal and Chinese demand. A liquid proxy for global growth sentiment.

Negara Penerbit
Australia
Bank Sentral
Reserve Bank of Australia (RBA)
Sesi Perdagangan Utama
Sydney 22:00–07:00 GMT
Kode
AUD

Catatan Trading

  • ▹Commodity-linked (iron ore, coal)
  • ▹Proxy for China exposure
  • ▹Risk-on currency
  • ▹Historically a carry target

Faktor Penggerak Utama

RBA decisionsChinese economic dataIron ore pricesAustralian employment & CPIGlobal risk appetite

History

Floated in 1983 and became the retail world's favorite growth/carry vehicle. Its fortunes track Asia's industrial cycle more than any other G10 currency.
