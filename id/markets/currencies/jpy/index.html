Japanese Yen

The third most traded currency and Asia's benchmark. Decades of ultra-low rates made the yen the world's favorite funding currency for carry trades.

Negara Penerbit
Japan
Bank Sentral
Bank of Japan (BOJ)
Sesi Perdagangan Utama
Tokyo 00:00–09:00 GMT
Kode
JPY

Catatan Trading

  • ▹Safe-haven flows in risk-off episodes
  • ▹Funding side of global carry trades
  • ▹Highly sensitive to US-Japan yield gap
  • ▹Historic intervention risk at extremes

Faktor Penggerak Utama

BOJ policy and yield curve control legacyUS Treasury yieldsRisk sentiment (Nikkei, global equities)MOF intervention rhetoricTrade balance

History

Floated in 1973, hit historic highs after the 1985 Plaza Accord, and spent decades under deflation-fighting ultra-easy policy that defined global carry trade cycles.
