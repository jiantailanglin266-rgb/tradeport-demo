Kalender Ekonomi

Rilis terjadwal yang menggerakkan mata uang — kapan terjadi dalam zona waktu Anda, apa yang diukur, dan mengapa pasar bereaksi.

Bagaimana tanggal ini dibuat: tanggal rapat bank sentral diambil langsung dari kalender resmi. Indikator dengan aturan yang dipublikasikan (misalnya payroll AS pada Jumat pertama) dihitung dari aturan itu. Rilis yang tanggalnya berubah tiap bulan ditampilkan tanpa tanggal — ikuti tautan jadwal resmi. Kami tidak memperkirakan tanggal.

Referensi indikator

Apa yang sebenarnya diukur setiap rilis, mengapa menggerakkan pasar, dan pasangan mana yang paling terdampak.

USD

🇺🇸

Non-Farm Payrolls (NFP)

Dampak tinggi
Apa yang diukur
The net change in paid US jobs outside farming, private households and non-profits, released with the unemployment rate and average hourly earnings.
Mengapa menggerakkan pasar
Employment drives the Fed's policy path. A large surprise repricess rate expectations within seconds, and the wage component often matters more than the headline because it feeds inflation.
Jadwal rilis
Monthly, usually the first Friday, 08:30 New York time.
USD/JPYEUR/USDXAU/USD

U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics · Jadwal resmi

🇺🇸

Initial Jobless Claims

Dampak sedang
Apa yang diukur
The number of people filing for unemployment benefits for the first time in the past week.
Mengapa menggerakkan pasar
The most frequent read on the US labour market. Single weeks are noisy, so traders watch the four-week average for turning points ahead of the monthly payrolls report.
Jadwal rilis
Weekly, Thursdays, 08:30 New York time.
USD/JPYEUR/USD

U.S. Department of Labor · Jadwal resmi

🇺🇸

Consumer Price Index (CPI)

Dampak tinggi
Apa yang diukur
The change in prices paid by US urban consumers. Core CPI strips out food and energy to show the underlying trend.
Mengapa menggerakkan pasar
In an inflation-fighting cycle CPI is the single most important release for the dollar, because it directly shapes how quickly the Fed can cut or must hike.
Jadwal rilis
Monthly, typically mid-month. The exact date changes each month — see the official BLS schedule.
USD/JPYEUR/USDXAU/USD

U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics · Jadwal resmi

🇺🇸

Core PCE Price Index

Dampak tinggi
Apa yang diukur
The Fed's preferred inflation gauge, measuring prices of personal consumption expenditure excluding food and energy.
Mengapa menggerakkan pasar
The Fed targets PCE, not CPI. When the two diverge, PCE is the one policy actually responds to — which is why a quiet CPI can still be followed by a market-moving PCE print.
Jadwal rilis
Monthly, usually late in the month. Check the BEA release schedule.
USD/JPYEUR/USD

U.S. Bureau of Economic Analysis · Jadwal resmi

🇺🇸

US GDP (quarterly)

Dampak sedang
Apa yang diukur
The total value of goods and services produced in the US, released in advance, second and third estimates.
Mengapa menggerakkan pasar
It is backward-looking, so the reaction is usually smaller than for CPI or payrolls — but a sharp miss changes the recession narrative and with it the expected rate path.
Jadwal rilis
Quarterly, with three revisions per quarter. See the BEA schedule.
USD/JPYEUR/USD

U.S. Bureau of Economic Analysis · Jadwal resmi

🇺🇸

US Retail Sales

Dampak sedang
Apa yang diukur
The change in the total value of sales at the retail level, including a 'control group' used in GDP.
Mengapa menggerakkan pasar
Consumption is roughly two thirds of the US economy, so retail sales is the fastest read on whether the consumer is still carrying growth.
Jadwal rilis
Monthly, usually mid-month. See the Census release calendar.
USD/JPYEUR/USD

U.S. Census Bureau · Jadwal resmi

🇺🇸

ISM Manufacturing PMI

Dampak sedang
Apa yang diukur
A survey of purchasing managers in manufacturing. Above 50 signals expansion, below 50 contraction.
Mengapa menggerakkan pasar
A forward-looking survey released early in the month, so it often sets the tone before the hard data arrives. The prices-paid sub-index is watched as an inflation signal.
Jadwal rilis
Monthly, on the first business day, 10:00 New York time.
USD/JPYEUR/USD

Institute for Supply Management · Jadwal resmi

EUR

🇪🇺

Eurozone HICP (flash estimate)

Dampak tinggi
Apa yang diukur
The harmonised measure of consumer price inflation across the euro area.
Mengapa menggerakkan pasar
The ECB's mandate is price stability, so HICP is the primary input to its rate decisions. National releases from Germany and Spain the day before often front-run the euro-area figure.
Jadwal rilis
Monthly flash estimate, at the end of the month.
EUR/USDEUR/JPYEUR/GBP

Eurostat · Jadwal resmi

GBP

🇬🇧

UK CPI

Dampak tinggi
Apa yang diukur
The change in consumer prices in the United Kingdom, including core and services inflation.
Mengapa menggerakkan pasar
Services inflation and wage growth are what the Bank of England has focused on, so the sub-components frequently move sterling more than the headline rate.
Jadwal rilis
Monthly, usually mid-month, 07:00 London time.
GBP/USDGBP/JPYEUR/GBP

Office for National Statistics · Jadwal resmi

JPY

🇯🇵

Japan CPI / Tokyo CPI

Dampak sedang
Apa yang diukur
Japanese consumer price inflation. The Tokyo area figure is released about three weeks ahead of the nationwide number.
Mengapa menggerakkan pasar
After decades of deflation, sustained Japanese inflation is what allows the Bank of Japan to normalise policy — which is the central question for the yen.
Jadwal rilis
Monthly. Tokyo CPI leads the national release by around three weeks.
USD/JPYEUR/JPY

Statistics Bureau of Japan · Jadwal resmi

CNY

🇨🇳

China Manufacturing PMI

Dampak sedang
Apa yang diukur
A survey of Chinese manufacturers; the official NBS index and the private Caixin index are both watched.
Mengapa menggerakkan pasar
China is the demand engine for industrial commodities, so the print moves the Australian and New Zealand dollars during Asian hours as much as it moves the yuan.
Jadwal rilis
Monthly, at the end of the month (NBS) and the first business day (Caixin).
AUD/USDNZD/USDUSD/CNY

National Bureau of Statistics of China · Jadwal resmi
