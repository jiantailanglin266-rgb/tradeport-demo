New Zealand Dollar

A dairy-export-driven currency and the smallest G10 major. Thinner liquidity outside Pacific hours produces sharper spikes on news.

Issuing Country
New Zealand
Central Bank
Reserve Bank of New Zealand (RBNZ)
Main Trading Sessions
Wellington/Sydney 21:00–06:00 GMT
Code
NZD

Trading Notes

  • ▹Dairy price sensitivity
  • ▹Thin liquidity off-session
  • ▹RBNZ known for early, bold moves
  • ▹High beta to risk sentiment

Key Drivers

Global dairy trade auctionsRBNZ decisionsChinese demandRisk appetite

History

The 'kiwi' floated in 1985 amid sweeping reforms. The RBNZ pioneered inflation targeting in 1990 — the framework now used by nearly every major central bank.
