Strategy Overview

Titan Momentum joins established intraday momentum on XAU/USD and US500 after volume-confirmed continuation signals, US session only.

Position size is scaled inversely to current volatility so dollar risk stays constant across calm and wild days.

Setup & Installation

  1. 1Verify your broker's contract size for gold and index CFDs first.
  2. 2Attach to H1 charts; enable US-session window.
  3. 3Start with 0.5% risk during the first demo month.

Recommended VPS: Standard VPS; stable connection during US session
