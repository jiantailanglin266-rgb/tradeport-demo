Meridian Swing

A patient H4/D1 swing system holding positions for days. Few trades, wide stops, high reward-to-risk targets. Suited to small accounts and traders who cannot watch charts.

Strategy Overview

Meridian Swing enters on higher-timeframe structure breaks with retest confirmation, targeting 2.5R+ moves over several days.

Positions take partial profit at 1R and trail the remainder behind swing structure. Low trade frequency (3–8 per month per pair) is by design.

Setup & Installation

  1. 1Attach to H4 charts of supported pairs.
  2. 2Defaults suit accounts from $300 with 0.01 lot precision.
  3. 3Set weekly close filter if you avoid weekend gaps.
  4. 4Review open positions once daily; no constant monitoring needed.

Recommended VPS: Any stable VPS; latency not critical
