Strategy Overview

Pulse Scalper fades short-term liquidity spikes during the London/New York overlap, targeting quick mean reversion of 5–10 pips with fixed stops.

A spread guard skips any signal when the current spread exceeds the configured cap, and a daily loss limit halts trading after a losing day.

Setup & Installation

  1. 1Use only on RAW/ECN account types with typical EUR/USD spread under 0.4 pips.
  2. 2Attach to M5 charts of EUR/USD and USD/JPY.
  3. 3Set MaxSpreadPips conservatively (default 0.6).
  4. 4Configure broker GMT offset for correct session detection.
  5. 5Verify slippage on demo before any live use.

Recommended VPS: Low-latency VPS (<10ms to broker) strongly recommended
