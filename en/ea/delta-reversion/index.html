Strategy Overview

Delta Reversion sells overextension and buys oversold conditions measured by Bollinger deviation plus RSI divergence, on low-volatility pairs where ranges dominate.

Every trade has a fixed stop and time-based exit. A volatility regime filter disables trading when ATR expands beyond its threshold.

Setup & Installation

  1. 1Best on EUR/GBP and USD/CHF M15.
  2. 2Keep the regime filter enabled — it is the core risk control.
  3. 3Avoid running during central bank weeks on the traded currencies.
