Corrections Policy

How TRADEPORT handles errors — because in financial content, corrections are a feature, not an embarrassment.

Updated: Jul 1, 2026

Reporting an error

Send the page URL, the claim in question and a supporting source to corrections@tradeport.example. Factual error reports are triaged within 3 business days.

How corrections appear

Material corrections are noted on the affected page with the date and nature of the change, and the 'updated' date is refreshed. Translations of a corrected page are flagged 'needs update' until re-reviewed.
