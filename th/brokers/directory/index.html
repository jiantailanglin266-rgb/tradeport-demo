Checklist

สิ่งที่ต้องตรวจก่อนฝากเงิน

สิบข้อที่กำหนดต้นทุนจริงและความเสี่ยงจริงของคุณ

  1. 1

    Spread type (fixed or variable)

    A fixed spread is predictable; a variable one is tighter when calm and can widen sharply. They suit different strategies.

  2. 2

    Commission per lot

    Quoted per side or round turn. Without it you cannot compare a raw-spread account against a commission-free one.

  3. 3

    Overnight swap rates

    Charged every night a position is held. Over days or weeks this often exceeds the spread you compared so carefully.

  4. 4

    Maximum leverage for your country

    Caps differ by regulator and by your residency, not by the headline figure on the homepage.

  5. 5

    Margin call and stop-out levels

    Determines when positions are force-closed. Two brokers with identical spreads can behave very differently here.

  6. 6

    Minimum deposit and lot size

    The smallest tradable size matters more than the deposit if you size positions by risk percentage.

  7. 7

    Regulator and licence number

    Check the number on the regulator's own register, not on the broker's site. The entity you contract with may differ by country.

  8. 8

    Client fund segregation and negative balance protection

    Whether your money is held separately, and whether you can lose more than you deposit.

  9. 9

    Withdrawal methods, fees and processing time

    The cost that is easiest to overlook and hardest to discover after you have deposited.

  10. 10

    Restricted countries and accepted residencies

    Some brokers cannot accept clients from your country at all, regardless of what the signup form allows.
