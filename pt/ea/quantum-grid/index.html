Quantum Grid

A range-market grid system with martingale-style lot scaling. High win rate, but drawdowns are deep and trending markets are its enemy. HIGH RISK — read the risk section before use.

Visão Geral da Estratégia

Quantum Grid places a ladder of counter-trend orders across a range, scaling lot size on deeper levels. Baskets close on aggregate profit targets.

An equity stop closes everything at a configured account drawdown. This cap is the single most important setting — without it, a strong trend can destroy the account.

Configuração e Instalação

  1. 1Only for ranging pairs (AUD/NZD recommended). Do not attach to trending instruments.
  2. 2Capital of $3,000+ per pair at minimum grid settings.
  3. 3Set EquityStopPct — never disable it.
  4. 4Suspend the EA ahead of major central bank events.
