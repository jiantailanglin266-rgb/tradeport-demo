How to Install an EA on MT4/MT5: Step-by-Step Setup Guide

From copying the file into the Experts folder to enabling Algo Trading and verifying the smiley icon — a complete, careful walkthrough for running your first Expert Advisor safely on a demo account.

Autor: M. RiveraPublicado: 2 de jun. de 2026Atualizado: 15 de jul. de 2026Dados verificados em: 15 de jul. de 2026

Pontos-Chave

  • ✓EA files (.ex4/.ex5/.mq4/.mq5) go into the platform's MQL4/Experts or MQL5/Experts folder.
  • ✓Enable 'Algo Trading' (AutoTrading) and allow automated trading in options.
  • ✓Attach the EA to the exact chart symbol and timeframe it was designed for.
  • ✓Always run new EAs on a demo account for weeks before any live deployment.
  • ✓A VPS keeps the EA running when your computer is off.

Sumário

How do you install the EA files?

Open your platform and choose File → Open Data Folder. Copy the EA file into MQL4/Experts (MT4) or MQL5/Experts (MT5), then restart the platform or right-click Refresh in the Navigator's Expert Advisors section.

In Tools → Options → Expert Advisors, enable 'Allow algorithmic trading'. The toolbar's Algo Trading (AutoTrading) button must also be switched on — it is the master switch for all EAs.

How do you attach and configure the EA?

Open a chart with the exact symbol and timeframe the EA is designed for, then drag the EA from the Navigator onto it. In the dialog, review the Inputs tab — set risk per trade, magic number and any session filters according to the EA's documentation.

After clicking OK, check the chart's top-right corner: a smiley face (MT4) or the EA name without a red mark (MT5) confirms it is running. A sad face or gray icon usually means Algo Trading is disabled.

How do you operate an EA safely?

Start on demo for at least several weeks and compare behavior against the documentation: trade frequency, stop placement and lot sizing should match expectations. Only then move to a small live account.

For continuous operation, run the platform on a VPS close to your broker's server. Monitor weekly, keep a log of parameter changes, and stop the EA around major news if its documentation advises it.

Perguntas Frequentes

›Why doesn't my EA open any trades?

Check in order: Algo Trading enabled, correct symbol/timeframe, market open, inputs valid for your account size, and the Experts/Journal tabs for error messages — they state the reason in most cases.

›Can I run multiple EAs at once?

Yes, on separate charts. Ensure each has a unique magic number so they manage only their own orders, and confirm combined risk stays within your account limits.

›Do I need a VPS?

For EAs that manage positions around the clock, yes — a home PC that sleeps or reboots can leave orders unmanaged. For signal-only or session-limited EAs it is optional.
